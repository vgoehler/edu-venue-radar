Calls lists each call of the edition (workshops, papers, special sessions, ...). A bold date is confirmed on the official page. “Typically” is the previous cycle's deadline moved to this cycle.

Languages are the languages a contribution may be submitted in, with talks and proceedings listed where they differ. A solid box means the call states it; a dashed one is inferred from the call text, programme or proceedings publisher. The filter keeps venues that accept at least one of the ticked languages.

ICORE (ICORE2026) rates the main paper track, A* > A > B > C. A workshop at an A conference is not an A publication. Education and German-language venues are not part of ICORE, so “not listed” says nothing about quality there.

Crawler shows what the weekly crawl saw: whether the page changed, future dates near the call keywords (hover for context; ✓ means it matches the confirmed deadline), whether the next edition's page is online, and “language?” when the page names a language or proceedings publisher the registry does not list (hover for the phrase). It never changes the confirmed data, that is edited in registry.yaml.
